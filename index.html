2. Pay-floor check: did you clear the legal minimum?

Some places guarantee app-based workers a minimum for engaged time and miles. The rates behind this tool (verified October 22, 2026)

  • IRS mileage, 2026: 72.5¢/mi (Jan 1–Jun 30), 76¢/mi (Jul 1–Dec 31) — IRS.
  • Self-employment tax: 15.3% on 92.35% of net profit; Social Security wage base $184,500 for 2026 — IRS / SSA.
  • California Prop 22: 120% of the minimum wage per engaged hour + $0.37 per engaged mile (2026) — CA DIR / CA State Treasurer.
  • New York City: $22.13 per hour of trip time, before tips, from April 1, 2026 — NYC DCWP.
  • Seattle: $0.47/min + $0.80/mile, $5.34 minimum per offer (2026) — Seattle Office of Labor Standards.
  • Washington rideshare: from Jan 1, 2026 — $0.70/min + $1.63/mile or $6.12/trip (trips starting in Seattle); $0.40/min + $1.38/mile or $3.55/trip (outside Seattle) — WA L&I.
